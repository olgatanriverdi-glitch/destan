3. What data we process

3.1 Game data on your device (device only)

Your gold and diamond balance, records, missions, chest and shop status, sound/layout settings and in-game saves are stored on your device in local preference storage (Unity PlayerPrefs). This data is not sent to us. Saves are protected against corruption and tampering by an integrity signature (HMAC). If you uninstall the app, this data is deleted from your device.

For the online leaderboard, temporary identity tokens (a Firebase ID token and a refresh token) and your nickname are also stored locally on your device. These tokens are used only for leaderboard requests.

  • About the diamond balance: Diamonds are a virtual balance valid only inside the game; they cannot be converted to real money and are not linked to an account. If you delete the app or change device, your balance may be lost; backup and restore behaviour depends on your operating system and device settings and we do not guarantee that it will be restored.

3.2 Online leaderboard (optional)

Data is sent only when you enter a nickname and join the leaderboard. Without a nickname, records are not sent to the server; they are kept on the device.

When processing requests, Firebase may process, on Google's side, technical information that arises naturally with every internet request, such as the IP address; its retention is subject to Google's own processes. This information is not passed on to us or to the in-game leaderboard.

The app does not collect or send location, contacts, camera, microphone, advertising ID or device ID. There is no third-party analytics/tracking SDK; Unity's Engine Diagnostics is turned off, so no crash or usage data is sent to Unity.

3.5 In-app purchases

The Android version has no in-app purchases and no payment data is processed. If they are added in the future, this policy will be updated.

3.6 Advertising

No ads are shown in the game and no requests are sent to any ad network. If an advertising provider is added in the future, this section will be updated to state the provider and the data collected (e.g. advertising ID).

3.7 Google Play components

The game content is installed via Google Play Asset Delivery. This library includes Google's own technical logging infrastructure (datatransport); it falls under Google's Play services and Google's privacy policy. The game does not send us data through this component.
